SURVEY BRIEFING

The Clarion-Clipperton Zone contains an estimated 21 billion tonnes of polymetallic nodules. Mining contractors need centimetre-accurate survey maps. The Oriel surveys at 600 m — below GPS range — navigating on INS + DVL, which drifts over time. Every node you collect is tagged with your estimated position.

Scoring

  • ● error < 2.5 km → HIGH quality (×3.0)
  • ● error < 4 km → MEDIUM quality (×1.5)
  • ● error ≥ 4 km → LOW quality (×0.5)

Node capture uses your estimated position — if you're lost, you miss.
